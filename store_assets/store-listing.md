## Full description

**Quran Teacher Family — Arabic-first Quran teaching for families**

Free with ads. Teach Quran at home with a calm, respectful Arabic learning companion. Built for parents who want to give their children structured Arabic Quran reading, listening, word-by-word practice, memorization, and progress tracking.

**Arabic is the center. English meaning is optional.**

Arabic Quran reading and recitation are the heart of this app. English meaning is available as optional support — hidden by default, shown when the learner needs help understanding.

**FEATURES**

📖 Full Arabic Quran Reader
All 114 surahs in clean Arabic text. Adjustable font size. Jump to any surah. Continue from where you left off.

🔊 Arabic Recitation Audio
Two recitation styles:
• Muallim (Al-Husary) — clear and educational
• Mujawwad (Al-Husary) — melodic with full Tajweed

ا Word-by-Word Arabic Teacher Mode
Teach each Arabic word. Play, repeat, highlight. Perfect for parent-supervised home teaching.

🔄 Repeat Practice
Select ayahs and repeat them with configurable count and delay. Build memorization through structured repetition.

📋 Daily Assignments
Create structured daily Quran tasks: read in Arabic, listen, word-by-word, memorization review.

📊 Memorization Tracker
Track every ayah per learner: Learning, Practicing, Needs Review, Memorized.

👤 Learner Profiles
Local profiles for each family member. No account or email required.

🔖 Bookmarks and Notes
Save ayahs. Add study notes. Stays on your device.

🔍 Search
Search surah names, Arabic text, and English meaning.

🌙 Optional English Meaning
Pickthall translation (public domain). Hidden by default. Never replaces Arabic learning.

**ABOUT ADS**

This app is free and supported by Google AdMob banner and limited interstitial ads.
- Banner ads appear at the bottom of non-Quran screens only.
- Interstitial ads are shown only at natural stopping points (completed assignment or practice session summaries). Maximum 3 per session and at most once every 10 minutes.
- Ads are never shown inside Quran text, between ayahs, during audio playback, in Teacher Mode, or during active practice.
- Ads are configured for a family-safe experience.
- Internet is required for ads and for loading Quran text and audio. Local learner data stays on your device.

**DISCLAIMER**

This app is a Quran learning companion. It does not replace a qualified Quran teacher, Tajweed instructor, imam, or Islamic scholar. English meaning does not replace the Arabic Quran.

Arabic text: Al-Quran Cloud (Uthmani, from Tanzil). English: Pickthall (public domain). Audio: Islamic Network CDN.
