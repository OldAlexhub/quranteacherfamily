Quran Teacher Family uses a server for content and general app analytics. Analytics are on by default for new installations and can be disabled in settings; existing opt-outs are preserved. Events use a resettable installation identifier and exclude learner names, passage history, notes, recordings, and personalization state. Server events expire after 90 days. Local learning data stays on the device. Google AdMob provides child-directed, nonpersonalized banners and limited session-end interstitials. See PRIVACYPOLICY.md for content, advertising, network, and deletion details.
