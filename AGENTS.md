# Product preferences

- General app analytics are enabled by default for new installations. Preserve saved opt-outs and provide an accessible off switch. Never include learner names/IDs, passage or recall histories, notes, recordings, or personalization state in analytics. Keep disclosures consistent with actual behavior.
- Preserve Arabic text as UTF-8. Do not write Unicode source through a shell pipeline that substitutes question marks; verify rendered Arabic after UI changes.
- Ads must remain outside Quran reading, tafsir, lessons, recall, and recitation, with child-directed treatment and the agreed session-end limits. Use test ad units for development.
