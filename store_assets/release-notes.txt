Version 1.0.0

Initial release of Quran Teacher Family.

- Full Arabic Quran reader (114 surahs)
- Word-by-word Arabic teacher mode
- Repeat practice with Muallim and Mujawwad recitation styles
- Memorization tracker per learner
- Daily assignments
- Local learner profiles
- Bookmarks and notes
- Search (surah names, Arabic text, English meaning)
- Progress reports and exports
- Optional English meaning (Pickthall, hidden by default)
- Three themes: Light, Warm, Dark
- Offline-first design — no internet required
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
