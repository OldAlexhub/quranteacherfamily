## Usage Notes

- Audio files are delivered via Google Play Asset Delivery.
- The application supports two recitation styles: Muallim Teacher Style and Mujawwad Tajweed Style.
- No audio is streamed from a remote server. All audio is bundled in asset delivery packs.
- This app does not claim or certify any user's Tajweed proficiency.
- This is a listening and repetition tool, not an automated Tajweed scoring system.

## Disclaimer

Audio recitation provided for learning and practice purposes only. This application does not replace a qualified Quran teacher, Tajweed instructor, or Islamic scholar. Parents and learners should seek qualified instruction for formal Quran study and memorization verification.
