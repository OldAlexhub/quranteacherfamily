# Pickthall English Translation — Public Domain Notes

## Translation

**The Meaning of the Glorious Koran** by Marmaduke Pickthall (1875–1936)

First published: 1930

## Public Domain Status

The Pickthall English translation of the Quran is in the public domain in the United States and in most countries where the copyright term has expired (typically 70–100 years after the author's death).

Marmaduke Pickthall died in 1936. This translation is therefore in the public domain in the United States under Project Gutenberg's guidelines, and in most other jurisdictions.

## Usage Notes

- This translation is labeled "English meaning" in the application, not as the primary Quran text.
- English meaning is optional and hidden by default.
- Users are encouraged to learn and understand the Arabic Quran text directly.
- The English meaning is provided only to aid comprehension, not to replace Arabic learning.
- This application does not claim the English meaning is a substitute for the Arabic Quran.

## Disclaimer

This translation is one scholar's interpretation of the meaning of the Arabic Quran. It is not a perfect rendering of Divine Speech. Users should refer to qualified scholars for religious guidance.
